Golf rules


Base version:


Playing deck:
Standard shuffled deck of 52 cards


Start:
Each player is dealt 6 random cards face down arranged in two rows with three column. The rest of the deck lays face down. A card is taken from the deck, and flipped face up in a new discard pile. Each player then flips any 2 cards of their choice face up.


The dealer places the leftover deck to the side as the go-fish pile (idk what else to call the deck lmao) and creates the discard pile by flipping over the topmost card of the go-fish pile


Turns:
Whoever dealt the cards is the first player to have a turn. The turns alternate after the opponent’s turn is completed.


The player who’s turn it is has 2 initial options and 3 sub options:


- 1. The player can flip one of their own face down cards over, then…
-
- 1a. The player can swap the newly face up card with the topmost card in the discard pile (known card)
- 1b. Or, the player can swap the newly face up card with the topmost card in the go-fish pile (unknown card)
- 1c Or, the player can avoid swapping cards by drawing the topmost card in the go-fish pile and placing onto the discard pile 
1. 2. The player can skip flipping one of their own face cards, then…
2.
- 2a. The player can swap any of their face up cards with the topmost card in the discard pile (known card)
- 2b. Or, the player can swap any of their face up cards with the topmost card in the go-fish pile (unknown card)
- 2c Or, the player can avoid swapping cards by drawing the topmost card in the go-fish pile and placing onto the discard pile


Card Face Values:
Ace is 1
3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, & 10 are their normal values
J & Q are 10


2 is -2
K is 0


Card Value Modifiers:
If any two cards of the face value are in a column, both cards’ value temporarily becomes 0 (if they’re moved such that the cards are no longer in a column, their value returns to their face value)

Joker Swap
Pulling a joker allows you to swap any two cards (even if they are locked from other rules) on the table including any card in a player’s hand, the topmost card in the discard pile, and the topmost card in the go-fish pile. Jokers can only be used once!


Base deck joker variation: if playing without a joker, any Queen-Queen or Jack-Jack pair in the same column activates the joker rule at the end of the player’s turn.


After adding jokers into the 52-card deck, there are two scenarios:

Suited Rows
If all cards in a row have the same suit, each card in the row is now equal to the 2nd largest card’s face value


Devil’s Row (666)
If a player gets three 6s in the same row, the cards are stuck in place and cannot be swapped out with any other cards for the rest of the game.


Gambler’s Row (777)
If a player gets three 7s in the same row at any point during the game, they divide their final score by 7
- If there’s one dice in play, the player rolls the dice and subtracts 2 x dice’s value
- If there’s two dice in play, the player rolls the dice and subtracts the sum of the die’s value


Roman 5s (maybe rename?)
If a player’s 6-card hand contains a V-pattern of cards with a value of 10, divide their final score by 3 (always round up).


King-Queen Column
If a player has a king-queen pair in a column of the same color, they have a combined score of 5. If they are a different color, they have  a combined score of 15


Beggars Will be Choosers
The player with the highest face value card gets to guess the card of the opponent’s card before they flip one over, if they guess correctly the player trades their highest face card value with the opponent’s lowest face value card. If both players are tied for having the highest face value card, neither gets the chance to guess.


4 of a Kind
If a player has four of the same face value card anywhere in their hand, the player’s gets the choice to move the cards into two columns immediately after reveal

Hold ‘em
If a players hand contains a flush (5 cards with the same suit), subtract 2 points from their score
If a players hand contains 5 cards with a straight, subtract 2 points from their score
If a players hand contains a 3 of a kind and a 2 of a kind, subtract 5 points from their score
If a players hand contains a 4 of a kind, subtract 5 points from their score
If a players hand contains a royal flush, subtract 10 points from their score


V10 rule
If you have a queen, jack, and a 10 in any order, in a V pattern, divide your final score by 5


Tricky straights
If you have an ascending straight in a row add the value of the rightmost card to your final score.
If you have a descending straight in a row, subtract the value of the rightmost card to your final score.


The ripped 5
If a player has a 5 of diamonds and a 5 of any other suit, in their hand in any position, subtract 5 to the final game score


House rules:
Joker rule: If playing with a deck of 54 (2 jokers) and a joker is drawn from the face-down pile, the player who drew it can switch the positions of any two cards. This includes the top card in the facedown deck, the top card in the discard pile, and any of their cards or their opponent’s cards. If the Joker is revealed as one of the player's cards, the rule activated as well. After the player has made a swap, their turn restarts.
