Privacy

Golf is a self-hosted card game. Google sign-in is optional; you can also play as a guest.

Accounts and game results

When you sign in, the server verifies your Google identity and stores Google's account identifier, an internal account ID, and the unique name you choose. It does not store your Google email address, photo, password, or Google access tokens. Display names can change between games.

Saved game summaries include player identifiers, unique and display names, scores, winners, rules selected, and rules activated. Match history and player stats are visible to everyone with access to this game server. Guest results are grouped by display name. Hidden card layouts are not stored in match history.

Cookies

The game uses a browser session cookie for your table and a separate cookie to remember Google sign-in for up to 30 days. Signing out revokes that login session. Login tokens are stored as hashes on the server. Emote and display-name preferences are saved in your browser.

Hosting and retention

The host stores accounts and results in a local database until they are deleted. Network and hosting services, including Cloudflare when used, process requests to deliver the game. Google processes sign-in under its own privacy policy. Contact the person hosting this game to request deletion of your account or results.
